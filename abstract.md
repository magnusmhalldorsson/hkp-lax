A finite graph is word-representable if and only if it admits a semi-transitive orientation. A representing word need not initially be uniform; the converse construction produces a positive uniform representation.

For a noncomplete word-representable graph with $n$ vertices and clique number $\omega$, the corrected HKP construction gives a representing word in which every vertex occurs exactly $2(n-\omega)$ times. Consequently its representation number is at most $2(n-\omega)$. The construction concatenates two-occurrence blocks associated with the vertices outside a maximum clique.
